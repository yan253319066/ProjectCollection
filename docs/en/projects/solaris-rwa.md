# Solaris RWA

> Decentralized real-world asset tokenization platform focusing on renewable energy.

Solaris RWA is an innovative RWA (Real World Asset) tokenization platform that converts solar, wind and other renewable energy assets into on-chain tokens, creating a sustainable green economy.

## Features

- **RWA tokenization** — Convert renewable energy assets to on-chain tokens
- **Sustainable investing** — Buy and trade RWA tokens for returns
- **Green energy** — Solar, wind and clean energy project support
- **On-chain transparency** — All data publicly verifiable
- **Decentralized** — Smart contract-managed assets and rewards

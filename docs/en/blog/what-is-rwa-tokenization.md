RWA (Real World Asset) tokenization is one of the fastest-growing sectors in Web3. Simply put, it converts physical or traditional financial assets into blockchain-based digital tokens.

## Problems RWA Tokenization Solves

| Issue | Traditional | RWA Tokenized |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| Liquidity | Real estate is highly illiquid | Tokens trade 24/7 |
| Accessibility | High minimum investment | Fractional ownership |
| Transparency | Opaque asset info | On-chain data |
| Cross-border | Complex settlement | Instant global transfer |

## How RWA Tokenization Works

1. **Asset valuation** — Professional appraisal and legal review
2. **Contract deployment** — Deploy ERC20/RWA standard contracts
3. **Token issuance** — Issue tokens representing asset ownership
4. **Secondary trading** — Tokens trade on secondary markets
5. **Dividend distribution** — Smart contracts auto-distribute returns

## Key Use Cases

### Renewable Energy Tokenization

This is where I specialize. Solar and wind farms package future revenue into tokens. Investors buy tokens to earn a share of energy production income.

**Solaris RWA** focuses on renewable energy asset tokenization.

### Real Estate Tokenization

A $1M property can be split into 10,000 tokens at $100 each, dramatically lowering the investment barrier.

### Commodities

Gold, oil, and agricultural products can have on-chain token representations for 24/7 global trading.

### Bonds & Credit

Corporate and government bond tokenization improves market efficiency and accessibility.

## Market Data

- **2024 RWA market size**: $12B+
- **2026 projected**: $50B+
- **Major entrants**: BlackRock, Franklin Templeton
- **YoY growth**: 200%+ in on-chain RWA TVL

## Challenges

- **Regulatory compliance** — Varies by jurisdiction
- **Off-chain trust** — On-chain value depends on real-world asset authenticity
- **Oracle dependency** — Reliable off-chain data feeds needed

## From SEO to GEO: Search Is Being Redefined

Traditional search engines (Google, Bing) return **lists of blue links** — you click, browse, and filter. AI search engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews) generate **compiled answers** — synthesizing multiple sources into a single response. Users get the answer without clicking through.

This shift is what **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** addresses.

> **GEO** is the practice of optimizing content so AI search engines cite and recommend it in their generated answers. It determines whether your content appears in a ChatGPT response, a Perplexity summary, or a Claude citation.

### Why GEO Matters More Than Ever

| Dimension | Traditional SEO | GEO |
|-----------|----------------|-----|
| Output | Link list | Compiled answer |
| User action | Click → Browse | Answer direct |
| Traffic model | 1 click = 1 visit | Cited ≠ Clicked |
| Optimization goal | Rank #1 | Get cited + recommended |
| Key factors | Keywords + backlinks | Structure + authority + verifiability |

Research shows that **57.9% of AI answers cite external sources** ([source: AI Search Citation Study](https://www.getciteflow.ai)), and citations are heavily concentrated on a small number of sites. Websites not cited by AI engines are invisible in the zero-click search era.

## The 8 Dimensions of GEO

Based on analysis of ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews, GEO optimization spans these dimensions:

### 1. Structured Data (Schema Markup)

AI models prefer extracting information from structured data. FAQ Schema, HowTo Schema, and Article Schema significantly boost citation probability.

- **FAQ Schema** — AI extracts Q&A pairs as answer sources
- **HowTo Schema** — Step-by-step content gets cited more
- **Product Schema** — Product info, pricing, reviews
- **BreadcrumbList** — Help AI understand site structure

### 2. LLMs.txt

`/llms.txt` is the `robots.txt` for the AI era. Placed at your site root, it provides AI systems with distilled documentation. OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, and others read this file to understand site content.

```
https://yoursite.com/llms.txt
https://yoursite.com/llms-full.txt
```

### 3. Content Authority & Citations

AI engines prefer content that **other authoritative sites have cited**. Building a citation network (cite-worthy content) is critical.

### 4. Verifiability

AI models favor content with **specific data, dates, statistics, and source citations**. Vague claims are cited far less than data-backed assertions.

### 5. Semantic HTML

Use `<article>`, `<section>`, `<nav>` and other semantic tags to help AI understand page structure. Clear heading hierarchy (h1 → h2 → h3) is essential.

### 6. Mobile Experience & Performance

AI crawlers evaluate page load speed and mobile experience. Core Web Vitals directly impact AI visibility.

### 7. Brand Authority

AI engines are more likely to cite well-known brands and industry authorities. Building brand recognition, earning industry coverage, and maintaining content consistency all help.

### 8. Multi-Format Content

Publishing the same content as **articles, videos, infographics, podcasts** increases citation probability. The broader your content format coverage in AI training data, the more citation opportunities.
